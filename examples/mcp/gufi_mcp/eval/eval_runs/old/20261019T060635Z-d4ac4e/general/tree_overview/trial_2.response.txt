**What stands out:**
- **grp05** uses about twice the space of any other group, but its file count is about the same as the others.
- **grp02** has the most files (2,175) but ranks fourth for space, so its files tend to be smaller.
- **grp03** has the deepest or widest directory tree (65 directories), even though its file count is average.
- **grp04** uses the least space by a wide margin (under 0.5 GiB), yet it still has close to 2,000 files. Its files are small on average, around 250 KB each.

The sizes are the total logical (apparent) bytes of the files, not the disk blocks they actually take up. I checked them a second way with `gufi_du --apparent-size`, and the per-group totals matched.
