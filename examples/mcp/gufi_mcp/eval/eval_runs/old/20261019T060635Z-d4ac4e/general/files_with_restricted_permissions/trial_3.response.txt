**3,993 regular files** in eval_master can't be read by other users. "Other users" here means anyone who isn't the owner and isn't in the file's group. Together these files hold about **3.63 GB** (3,634,805,599 bytes).

I selected files whose "others" permission set has no read bit. They all have one of two permission settings:

| Mode | Meaning | Files | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `-rw-r-----` (0640) | owner can read and write, group can read, others can't | 2,973 | 2,279,815,649 |
| `-rw-------` (0600) | only the owner can read or write | 1,020 | 1,354,989,950 |

So 1,020 of them can be read only by their owner.
